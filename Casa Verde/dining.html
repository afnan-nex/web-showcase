The Culinary Universe

Flavors of Rainforest & Wild Pacific

Every dish tells a story of our biodynamic soil, indigenous wild botanicals, and line-caught ocean harvests. Curated by acclaimed Executive Chef Mateo Varela.

Signature Gastronomy

Terra

Farm-to-Table Gastronomy & Botanical Tasting Menus

Enveloped by torchlit reflecting pools and ancient rainforest trees, Terra celebrates hyper-local culinary artistry. Tasting menus evolve with the micro-seasons of our 8-acre biodynamic farm, paired with rare organic vintages from our sommelier cellar.

Hours: Breakfast 7:00 – 11:00 AM | Dinner 6:30 – 10:30 PM

Dress Code: Resort Elegant

Chef: Executive Chef Mateo Varela (Formerly Michelin 3-Star)

Sunset Oceanfront Lounge

Sol y Sombra

Cliffside Crudo Bar & Sunset Mezcal Lounge

Perched right on the dramatic cliff edge where crashing waves meet Pacific sunsets. Sol y Sombra features fresh wild ceviches, charcoal-grilled seafood skewers, and artisanal cocktails infused with native botanical cordials and rare agave distillates.

Hours: Daily 12:00 PM – Midnight

Dress Code: Smart Resort Casual

Lead Mixologist: Sofia Sterling

Daylight Sanctuary

The Botanical Greenhouse

Artisanal Breakfast, Cold-Pressed Elixirs & Afternoon Tea

A sunlit atrium enveloped by rare tropical orchids and living moss walls. Start your morning with cold-pressed wellness shots, acai bowls, and fresh sourdough pastries, or join us for a traditional botanical high tea service.

Hours: Daily 6:30 AM – 4:30 PM

Dress Code: Casual Resort

Master Pastry Chef: Laurent Mercier
