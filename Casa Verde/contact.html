Seamless Arrival

Getting to Casa Verde

Choose between scenic aerial transfers or private chauffeured coastal transit.

Private Helicopter

25 minutes direct flight from Juan Santamaría Int'l (SJO) to the Casa Verde private helipad. Enjoy panoramic views of the coastal rainforest.

Private Range Rover Chauffeur

Luxury private chauffeur transfer equipped with chilled towels, organic refreshments, and Wi-Fi. (Approx 2.5 hours scenic drive from SJO).

Private Yacht Arrival

Private deep-water yacht mooring available in our protected cove with private launch tender and dedicated butler dockside greeting.
