Our Healing Philosophy

Rooted in Indigenous Botanical Alchemy

We believe that true healing begins when the nervous system reconnects with the circadian rhythms of the living earth.

Our therapies harness locally wildcrafted ingredients: pure volcanic sulfur muds from the nearby Arenal caldera, cold-pressed copal and ylang-ylang resins, and single-origin raw cacao. Every treatment is preceded by a personalized botanical herbal infusion ceremony.
