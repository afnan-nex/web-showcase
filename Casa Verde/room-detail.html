The Residence Experience

Perched majestically on the volcanic cliff edge, this villa commands endless panoramic vistas of the azure ocean. Bask in dramatic sunsets from your private infinity pool or unwind in the open-air sunken lounge.

Designed in harmony with Costa Verde’s natural contours, the architecture blends hand-hewn cedar, floor-to-ceiling retractable glass, and artisanal limestone masonry. Natural ocean breezes circulate through passive cooling louvered panels, creating an effortlessly serene sanctuary.
