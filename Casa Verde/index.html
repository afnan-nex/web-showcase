The Casa Verde Philosophy

An Architecture of Silence & Wild Splendor

Conceived by visionary tropical modernists, Casa Verde is more than a luxury resort; it is a sacred dialogue between human serenity and untamed nature.

Every private villa is cantilevered into the volcanic cliffside or elevated above the emerald rainforest canopy, utilizing sustainable reclaimed cedarwood, local volcanic stone, and expansive open glass walls that invite the sea breeze inside.

Gastronomy of the Rainforest

Michelin-Inspired Flavors from Soil to Sea

Led by Executive Chef Mateo Varela, our culinary ethos centers on hyper-local biodynamic agriculture from our 8-acre organic gardens and daily catches by artisan coastal fishermen.

  • Terra: Farm-to-table tasting menus with sommelier wine pairings.
  • Sol y Sombra: Cliffside crudo bar and sunset mezcal mixology lounge.
  • The Botanical Greenhouse: Artisanal pastries and cold-pressed botanical elixirs.
