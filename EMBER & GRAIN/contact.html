Guest Etiquette & Booking Guidance

We recommend Elegant Smart Casual. We request that guests refrain from athletic wear, beachwear, and flip-flops to preserve the sophisticated atmosphere for all diners. Collared shirts or jackets are encouraged for gentleman in the Reserve Cellar Vault.
Standard dining reservations open exactly 30 days in advance at 9:00 AM EST every morning through our digital booking portal. For private buyouts or parties over 8 guests, please reach out via our private dining form up to 6 months in advance.
Yes, our culinary team takes allergies very seriously. We offer comprehensive Gluten-Free, Vegetarian, and Dairy-Free adaptations for almost all wood-fired dishes. Please note your dietary requirements during booking so Chef Mercer's team can prepare dedicated station prep.
Standard table reservations may be cancelled without penalty up to 24 hours prior to the seating via the "My Reservations" tab. Chef's Counter ($50 hold/guest) and Cellar Vault ($100 hold/guest) seatings require 48 hours notice to avoid a late cancellation charge.
Guests may bring up to two 750ml bottles of personal wine that are not currently represented on our 1,800-label reserve wine list. The corkage service fee is $65 per bottle, which includes professional decanting and varietal-specific crystal stemware service.
